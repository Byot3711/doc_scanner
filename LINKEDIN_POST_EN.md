🚀 I just launched Doc Scanner Pro — an Android app for scanning documents right from your phone.

Why I built it: I wanted a fast document scanner with no ads and no mandatory account, that pulls text out of photos (OCR) and turns them straight into a searchable PDF, ready to send.

What it does:
📸 Camera scan with auto-crop, or import straight from your gallery
🔤 Text extraction from images (OCR)
📄 PDF generation with the extracted text overlaid, fully searchable
⬇️ Download and 🖨️ Print directly from the app
🕓 Local scan history
🌗 Light / Dark mode

Stack: Flutter + Dart, Material 3, SQLite for local history.

The code is open-source on GitHub, and the APK is available straight from Releases — installs in a few seconds:
🔗 https://github.com/Byot3711/doc_scanner

Feedback is always welcome — still actively improving it, more updates coming. 🙌
